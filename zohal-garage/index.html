Zohal Garage support

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to games@performacreative.com. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

  • Where is my progress saved? Only on your iPhone. Deleting the game deletes your progress.
  • How do I switch the language? Tap the language button at the top of the cover. The game restarts in the other language and keeps your progress.
  • Do I need an internet connection or an account? No. The game works offline and has no account, ads or in-app purchases.
  • The sound is too loud or too quiet. Use the speaker button to mute, and the Workshop button to set the garage background to Off, Low or Normal.
