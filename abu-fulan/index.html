Abu Fulan support

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to games@performacreative.com. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

  • Where is my progress saved? On your phone. Deleting the game deletes your progress.
  • Do I need an account? No. Only to post a run on the leaderboard, with Apple or Google.
  • How do I delete my leaderboard account? Settings, "Delete my account" (احذف حسابي).
